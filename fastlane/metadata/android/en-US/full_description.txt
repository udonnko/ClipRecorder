ClipRecorder is a free and open-source video recorder for Android that focuses on clip management and editing — with no ads, no tracking, and no cloud dependency.

<b>Recording</b>
• Portrait and landscape recording
• Slow motion and timelapse modes
• Countdown timer before recording starts
• Optional GPS tagging in video metadata
• Home screen widget to start recording instantly

<b>Clip Management</b>
• List and grid thumbnail views with duration display
• Drag-and-drop reordering
• Correct portrait/landscape thumbnail orientation

<b>Editing</b>
• 16 video effects: Grayscale, Sepia, Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Warm, Cool, Vivid, Matte, Film Grain, Chromatic Aberration, Vignette, Cinematic, Ken Burns, Fade In/Out
• Background music (BGM) mixing
• GIF export

<b>Title Cards</b>
• Generate video title cards with custom text, subtitle, and background color
• Configure duration (1–15 sec), orientation, and text color
• Add generated title cards to the clip list, reorder and merge them

<b>Merging</b>
• Merge multiple clips in a custom order
• Preview clips before merging
• Reorder with timeline reset

<b>Privacy</b>
• No internet permission
• No analytics or crash reporting
• All processing done on-device
